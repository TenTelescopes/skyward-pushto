Skyward camera star test

This page checks one thing: how many stars your phone's back camera can pick up from a web page. It finds the longest single exposure the phone will give, and counts the stars in it. Nothing is uploaded; the pictures never leave your phone.

  1. Go outside on a clear night and give the phone a minute away from bright lights.
  2. Rest the phone against something solid with the back camera aimed at a starry patch of sky. Hand-held will blur.
  3. Tap Start camera and allow camera access.
  4. Tap Take the longest single frame and keep still. It spends up to half a minute slowing the camera down step by step to find the longest exposure your phone allows, then counts the stars in one frame.
  5. Tap Copy report and paste it into the Ten Telescopes Discord, or email it to admin@tentelescopes.com.

The second button opens the phone's normal camera, which can use its night mode for a much longer exposure than a web page is allowed. Take the photo, tap Use Photo, and the stars in it are counted the same way.
