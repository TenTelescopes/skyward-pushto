Skyward camera star test

This page checks one thing: how many stars your phone's back camera can pick up from a web page. It adds short frames together until it has two seconds of light, then counts the stars. Nothing is uploaded; the pictures never leave your phone.

  1. Go outside on a clear night and give the phone a minute away from bright lights.
  2. Rest the phone against something solid with the back camera aimed at a starry patch of sky. Hand-held will blur.
  3. Tap Start camera and allow camera access.
  4. Tap Take 2-second stack and keep still until it finishes.
  5. Tap Copy report and paste it into the Ten Telescopes Discord, or email it to admin@tentelescopes.com.
